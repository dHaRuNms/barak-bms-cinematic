Intelligent Battery Systems
In development

Battery intelligence engineered for real-world mobility.

Our Battery Management System is being developed for electric vehicles, commercial fleets, and energy storage applications where functional safety, availability, and service life are critical.

The system is intended to provide manufacturers and operators with deeper visibility into battery state, more reliable protection strategies, and a stronger control foundation for the next generation of electric products.

  • Improved observability of battery health, state, and performance
  • Earlier detection of abnormal operating conditions
  • Support for more predictable battery utilization and service life
  • Architecture informed by Indian operating conditions and local product development
  • A foundation for dependable vehicle, charger, and fleet-system integration

Built with these teams in mindElectric vehicle manufacturers, battery pack companies, fleet operators, energy storage providers, and industrial equipment manufacturers.

Battery Emulation & Simulation
Product in development

Validate more system behavior before committing to hardware.

We are developing engineering software that enables teams to model battery behavior, evaluate control strategies, and exercise demanding operating scenarios in a controlled digital environment.

The objective is to reduce avoidable hardware iterations, expose system-level issues earlier, and create a more traceable path from concept definition to technical validation.

  • Evaluate a broad range of electrical, thermal, and operating conditions
  • Support safer and more repeatable engineering workflows
  • Correlate simulation results with physical validation activities
  • Compress the cycle from design hypothesis to functional prototype

Product in development

An intelligent engineering environment for electronics development.

Barak is exploring a new generation of AI-assisted tools for circuit design and printed circuit board development. The product vision is to reduce repetitive engineering effort while giving engineers more time for architecture, trade-off analysis, and product-defining decisions.

From early design exploration through documentation, review, and iteration, the platform is intended to make hardware development more systematic, collaborative, and efficient.

  • Accelerate the transition from design intent to implementation
  • Establish more structured and traceable engineering workflows
  • Automate or assist repetitive design and documentation activities
  • Provide a flexible foundation for modern electronics development

Connected Mobility & Fleet Software

Operational intelligence for connected mobility networks.

Our mobility software integrates rider experiences, driver operations, fleet workflows, and connected vehicle services into an adaptable operational platform.

It is designed for the constraints of urban and commercial mobility: dependable booking, coordinated operations, fleet-level visibility, and an architecture that can expand as the network scales.

  • Frictionless booking and rider interaction flows
  • Purpose-built tools for drivers, owners, and fleet teams
  • Near real-time visibility into operational activity
  • Extensible foundations for electric and commercial mobility

Barak Microelectronics is an India-based technology company developing the systems infrastructure behind next-generation mobility and intelligent hardware.

We operate across energy management, electronics engineering, artificial intelligence, and connected operations. Our objective is to help manufacturers, engineering teams, and fleet operators identify risk earlier, accelerate technical decisions, and deliver products with greater confidence.

We are building a product portfolio rather than a single-purpose solution: intelligent battery management, battery emulation and simulation, AI-assisted engineering workflows, and software infrastructure for modern mobility networks.

Our mission

To make advanced energy and electronics technology more accessible, dependable, and effective in real operating environments.

Our vision

A future in which batteries, electronic systems, and vehicles are designed with greater technical clarity, operated with greater confidence, and continuously improved through intelligent software.
