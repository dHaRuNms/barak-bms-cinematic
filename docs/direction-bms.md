# BMS cinematic direction

THESIS: Follow one battery intelligence system from its circuit board to a connected fleet and a usable dashboard, in a single continuous camera space.
OWN-WORLD: Matte black studio, green solder mask, brushed aluminium, soft cyan rim light, restrained mint interface accents, locally hosted Manrope typography.
STORY: Understand the connection from cells to vehicles; inspect the simulated dashboard; request a demo through the preserved company contact flow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large left-aligned headline; floating detailed board across the right; quiet navigation above; seven-part scroll track below. The board reveals its real-time geometry as loading completes.
FORM: User-directed seven-stage product journey. The primary signature interaction is the reversible, continuous camera path driven by one normalized timeline. User explicitly authorized autonomous asset generation and completion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

All raster scenes and GLB models are authored for this task. This is conceptual hardware, never represented as production CAD. Simulated telemetry is labeled. No external asset service is required at runtime.

The initial asset and camera pass exposed asynchronous screenshot capture before the scrub completed; captures now wait on the camera's reported normalized progress. The renderer also lowers effects on sustained slow frame rates and stops continuous drawing offscreen.
